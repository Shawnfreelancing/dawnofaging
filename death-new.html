Death Doula Services

Comprehensive End-of-Life Planning ▶
  • Qualified Advance Care Facilitator: Clarify your wishes and create a plan to explore your values, preferences, and priorities to ensure your voice is heard when important decisions need to be made.
  • Legacy & Life Story Preservation: Capture memories, values, traditions, and personal stories through meaningful projects designed to celebrate your life and the impact you leave behind.
  • Family Communication & Transition Support: Facilitate honest conversations and provide guidance that helps families feel more prepared, connected, and supported through aging and end-of-life transitions.
  • Organize Essential Documents & Information: Create a clear roadmap with advance directives, important records, emergency information, and resources your loved ones can easily access.
Vulnerability Vault: Emotional and Spiritual Support ▶
  • Offer a safe and compassionate space for exploring emotions, fears, and spiritual concerns that may me taboo with others in your circles.
  • Vulnerability Vault: A place to Spill Secrets, Regrets and "What-ifs". A neutral party and an understanding ear for you to say all the things you can't say to others.
    • Swedish Death Cleaning Coach ▶
  • Leave a Legacy not a Mess: Make intentional choices about your possessions so your values, memories, and wishes are honored.
  • Preserve What Matters Most: Identify meaningful keepsakes, document family stories, and ensure treasured items find the right home.
  • Reduce the Burden on Loved Ones: Organize and simplify your belongings so your family isn't left with overwhelming decisions later.
  • Learning to live life with purpose in your posessions and less clutter leads to a safer environment as we age.
    • Vigil Sitting Services/ Respite Care ▶
  • Compassionate Presence During End-of-Life: Providing calm companionship, emotional support, and a reassuring presence for individuals and families during the final stages of life.
  • Support for Families and Caregivers: Offering respite, allowing loved ones time to rest, step away briefly, or care for their own needs while knowing someone is present.
  • A Peaceful, Meaningful Experience: Helping create an environment of comfort, dignity, connection, and support through conversation, storytelling, music, reflection, and simply being there.
    • Death Cafe Host & Community Workshops ▶
  • Open Conversations About Aging & End-of-Life: Creating safe, approachable spaces to discuss topics often avoided, including planning, caregiving, grief, legacy, and personal wishes.
  • Workshops for Obituary Writing, Legacy Letters, Emergency Binders and Advance Directives.
  • Educational Programs for Individuals & Organizations: Offering engaging conversationals and presentations designed for senior communities, caregivers, businesses, and community groups.
  • Public Speaking Engagements, Death Cafes, and Death/Dementia Deck Discussions for Senior programs and family support groups.
