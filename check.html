“People who need help sometimes look a lot like people that don't need help”

A guided planning experience for individuals and families preparing for the future with clarity, confidence, and peace of mind. Life transitions rarely happen all at once. Aging, caregiving, downsizing, illness, and end-of-life planning are emotional and complicated processes that require more than a checklist. Through my comprehensive Life Transition Support Package, I provide compassionate guidance, practical organization, and meaningful conversations over an initial month-long journey designed to help you create a plan that reflects what matters most.

Together, we will organize important information, explore wishes and priorities, preserve your story, and create a roadmap that reduces uncertainty for you and the people you love. This is not about preparing for the end, but creating more peace for the life you are living now.

PHASE ONE: Discover and Understand. Your Stories. Your Wishes. Your Priorities.

PHASE TWO: Organize and Prepare. Create Order Before It Becomes Urgent.

PHASE THREE: Simplify and Preserve. Keeping What Matters. Releasing What Doesn't.

PHASE FOUR: Making Sure Your Story Continues.

Where Are You in Your Life Transition?

Are you 55 and preparing for retirement, wanting to create a plan before the next chapter begins?

Are you 75 and ready to organize your affairs, simplify your life, and make things easier for the people you love?

Are you a family member whose parent has recently received a difficult diagnosis and you suddenly find yourself wondering, "Where do we even begin?"

At Dawn of Aging, I provide compassionate guidance, practical organization, and meaningful conversations to help individuals and families prepare for the future with clarity and confidence. Through personalized planning, legacy preservation, Swedish Death Cleaning, caregiver support, and end-of-life preparation, I help you transform uncertainty into understanding and overwhelm into meaningful conversations.

Because the best time to prepare for life's biggest transitions is before they become emergencies.

Note: This is a Private Pay NON-Medical Service. Insurance is not accepted. Due to medical information potentially being exchanged, we are HIPAA Compliant and care about your privacy.
